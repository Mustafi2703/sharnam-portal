# What's new — Sharnam portal

**August 2026**

---

## In one sentence

One online workspace for your construction project — drawings, quality, safety, site logs, meetings, cost, reports, and HR — aligned to SPDC ISO folders and your Excel registers.

---

## Highlights

| Area | What you get |
|------|----------------|
| **Login** | Four portals — Office, Site, Contractor, Client — professional sign-in on construction imagery |
| **Documents** | Full document manager (DMS) plus separate GFC drawing register |
| **Site** | Attendance with selfie + GPS + **IST time** → SharePoint |
| **Quality & Safety** | Separate tools — QI, NCR, cube register, safety observations |
| **Progress** | S-curve, summary schedule, procurement plan for client view |
| **Cost** | BOQ monitoring, MB, BBS, and **three cashflow views** (Chart · Forecast · Tracking) |
| **Reports** | DPR / WPR from live data; branded PDF print |
| **HRMS** | Recruitment through attendance, leave, payslips |
| **SharePoint** | Live file storage at `SharnamProjects` site |

---

## Cashflow (three tools)

Your Excel cashflow dashboard has three views. The portal keeps them separate:

1. **Cash Flow Chart** — month-wise visual  
2. **Cash Flow Forecast** — forward projection  
3. **Cashflow Tracking** — line-level follow-up  

Find them under **Cost** → Cashflow.

---

## What is not mixed up

| Keep separate | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| Documents vs Drawings | General files ≠ GFC revision control |
| Cost vs Finance | Engineering measurement ≠ commercial invoices |
| Ask vs Inspection | Drawing clarification ≠ quality/safety inspection |
| Field day log vs HR diary | Project record ≠ personal employee diary |
