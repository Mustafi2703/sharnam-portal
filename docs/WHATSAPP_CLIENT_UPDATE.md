*Sharnam Portal — Update and How To Use*
Portal: https://portal.spdc.in

Hello team, the CRM, HRMS, Quality and Safety modules are live. Here are the steps for each.

*1. CRM (Office login)*
1. Open *CRM → Clients*. Add the client once; one client can have many projects.
2. Add *Consultants* and *Vendors / Contractors* in their tabs.
3. *Projects → + New Project* opens the project card. Add the client, consultants, contractors and the *SPDC Team*.
4. Use *Activate Portal* on a client, consultant or vendor to give them a login for their projects only.
5. Each person sees only the projects they are added to.

*2. HRMS (HR / Office login)*
1. *Masters → Departments And Roles*: add or delete departments and roles as needed.
2. *Recruitment*: Requisition → Resumes → Interviews and Scorecards → Offers.
3. *Offers*: fill one form (role, joining date, CTC, letter details) → *Save offer and generate letter*. The SPDC Offer Letter and Annexure I are created from the same figures.
4. Mark *Sent* → *Candidate accepted* → finish the *Pre-joining checklist* → *Generate appointment letter* (filed in the employee's HR folder).
5. *Create portal login*: the joinee becomes a user. Share the one-time password privately; manage the login under *Users*.
6. *Attendance → Site locations*: pin each site on the map and set a radius. A check-in inside the radius is verified automatically. A site saved with only a name goes to HR for review.
7. *Attendance → Review & hours*: verify or reject locations, delete wrong check-ins or check-outs, and download the month log (hours for payslips).
8. Selfies are kept for one day only.

*3. Quality (Project → Quality)*
1. *Quality Assurance Plan*: *Upload sheet* (Week 50 format), or *+ Add Section / + Add Row* with every column. Pick-lists are given; typing your own text also works.
2. *Download XLSX* gives the Sharnam-branded QAP with drop-downs and Yes/No colours. It can be uploaded back.
3. *Inspection register*: Request for Inspection (F-01) and Activity Inspection (F-02), with Excel forms.
4. *QI & Checklist Fills*: fill, sign, download the branded PDF / Excel. Office can delete wrong fills.
5. *CAR / NCR, Site Observation, Cube Test*: registers with Excel and PDF downloads.
6. Files are filed in SharePoint under ISO 08.

*4. Safety (Project → Safety)*
1. Safety register and Safety Inspection Requests.
2. Safety checklists: assign, fill with photos and signatures, download the branded copy.
3. Registers and dashboard export to Sharnam-branded Excel / PDF.

*Also*
- Drawings: every discipline can now be picked on upload. The Master Register and GFC log are updated on every upload.
- RFIs: Office can delete test RFIs from the log. Delete the SharePoint copy by hand.

Please run through these and send us any issue with a screenshot. Emails are on hold until UAT sign-off.
