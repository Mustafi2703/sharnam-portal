1. Position, reporting and place of work — You are appointed as {{designation}} in {{department}}. Your services may be transferred or deputed to any SPDC office or project site in India. You shall devote your whole time and professional ability to the Firm and shall not engage in any other employment without prior written consent.

2. Remuneration — You shall be paid compensation as set out in Annexure I, subject to statutory deductions including income tax, Provident Fund, Professional Tax and ESIC where applicable. Salary shall be credited to your designated bank account on or before the 7th of the succeeding month.

3. Performance management — The Firm operates an annual performance cycle. Salary revision and performance pay are discretionary and not guaranteed.

4. Probation and confirmation — You shall be on probation for {{probationMonths}} months from {{joinDate}}, extendable by up to three months. Confirmation shall be communicated in writing on satisfactory completion of probation.

5. Hours, attendance and leave — Normal office hours apply; site postings follow project shift patterns. Leave shall be as per the Firm's leave policy and Annexure II.

6. Statutory benefits — You shall be enrolled under applicable labour laws including PF and ESIC as per eligibility. All deductions authorised by law or by you shall be made from salary.

7. Site deputation and travel — Site presence, client premises and vendor works may be required. Reimbursements follow the Firm's travel and expense policy.

8. HSE & quality — You shall comply with SPDC HSE, quality and ethics policies on every assignment.

9. Background verification — Joining is subject to satisfactory BGV, medical fitness and verification of identity, education and prior employment records.

10. Code of conduct — You shall comply with the SPDC code of conduct and anti-bribery policy at all times.

11. Confidentiality & IP — All client, project and Firm information is confidential. Work product created during employment belongs to the Firm.

12. Non-solicitation — During employment and for twelve (12) months thereafter you shall not solicit Firm clients or employees.

13. Notice & termination — Either party may terminate with one (1) month notice during probation and two (2) months thereafter, or payment in lieu, subject to Clause 13 of the full policy.

14. POSH — The Firm maintains a zero-tolerance policy on sexual harassment as per the applicable Act.

15. Data privacy — Personal data shall be processed per the Firm's privacy policy and applicable law.

16. Retirement — Superannuation age and benefits as per Firm policy and applicable law.

17. General — This letter, Annexures I–III and HR policies on the portal constitute the entire agreement. Amendments shall be in writing only.
